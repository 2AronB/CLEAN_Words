## Bee opening score B1 — activated 2026-10-26

Owner requested all approved Bee changes integrated, then explicitly selected
"Activate scoring now without an additional ratio filter". This supersedes the
side-task gate and rejected 60% floor. New daily/additional Bee puzzles use
bee-opening10-v3 with full ENABLE acceptance and existing base familiarity input.
No optional AI supplement is loaded. Original base board filters remain; there
is no additional ratio rejection or narrowed accepted-word list. Cross-board
score fairness and vocabulary familiarity remain provisional.

First ten distinct accepted words earn existing points (4 letters=1, longer=length,
pangram+7). Opening score = those points / points from that frozen board's best
ten accepted words * 100, half-up to two decimal places. Before ten it is labeled
provisional; after ten the choices/score cannot change. Invalid/duplicate guesses
consume no slot. Continued words add all-word points and equal word-goal credit.
The independent completion goal remains ceil(70% of frozen familiar count).

Opening recording and word-goal completion are independent. Both must finish for
a new scored daily to leave the prior unfinished-puzzle resume flow. Home, hub,
history and calendar distinguish them; calendar stays unfinished until both are
done. completedAt still records first word-goal achievement, never tenth-word
recording. No extra stored score/timestamp or save-schema change is introduced.
Ordered submissions/edit revisions persist; malformed/stale/reordered edits are
refused without overwriting old state/results. Old v1/v2 snapshots keep their
rules and cannot acquire an inferred opening. A saved older daily wins over new
generation; Another puzzle immediately tries the new scoring mode.

Generator/source/profile/benchmark/rounding, ratio 0 and explicit owner approval

## Proposed S1 baseline — Five-game offline playable baseline

Prepared and APPROVED 2026-10-25 by "You're good to proceed," with TEN starter
puzzles per game, future-development/difficulty planning and meaningful boundary
checks. This is an internal playable milestone across all five games, not full
MVP completion. Named S1 and its standard dependencies/build downloads are

### Flow and common rules

- Open a five-game home list; choose a game and its daily or additional puzzle.
  Use consistent help, Back, result and history conventions with game-specific
  boards/input. Temporary development names: tWordle, Connektions, Speling Bee,
  Stranded and Crossita; R-007 release rename remains mandatory.
- Use local device date with a 6 a.m. boundary: before 6 a.m. belongs to the prior
  calendar date. Reopening the same game/date resumes the same puzzle. Changing
  the phone clock/time zone recalculates the current date but never deletes or
  replaces saved attempts. No anti-cheating clock service.
- Calendar shows saved daily attempts and their not-finished/completed/failed
  status. Missed dates have no attempt; S1 does not supply an unplayed archive.
  Below each current daily, the resume option opens the most recent unfinished
  prior daily; calendar reaches the others. Old completion keeps the original
  puzzle date and records its actual finish time. Streaks are deferred.
- Save game progress automatically, including guesses, selected/entered letters
  and found answers. Back and normal interruption preserve progress; reopen
  restores it. Completed/failed attempts are viewable without resubmitting or
  overwriting results. Additional attempts have separate identities/history.
- Requesting another puzzle creates an additional attempt. Preserve any previous
  unfinished attempt rather than silently replacing it. S1 history permits resume
  of unfinished additional attempts. No delete/reset feature in this slice.
- Daily content selection is stable by game/date. Bundle at least ten original,
  mechanically checked puzzle records per game; extra play cycles through this
  sample pool with a visible development-content/repetition notice. Daily and
  extra puzzles may repeat content but never share mutable progress. No claim
  of abundant unique content or calibrated 3.5/5 difficulty for S1.
- Rules explain accepted words and completion. Include offline credits for any
  reused material. Invalid input gives a reason without losing prior progress.
  No accounts, ads, tracking, internet permission, online features or paid SDKs.
- History records game, daily/additional mode, puzzle identity/version/date,
  start/finish times, current status and game-specific result. Preserve puzzle
  content and rules needed to resume or interpret old attempts after updates.
  No export/import, cloud/device backup promise or cross-device recovery in S1.

### Minimum playable game rules (approved S1 defaults)

| Game | S1 behavior |
| --- | --- |
| tWordle | One five-letter answer, six valid guesses, exact/present/absent feedback with duplicate letters counted correctly. Invalid/short guesses consume no turn; win on exact answer, fail and reveal after six incorrect valid guesses. Separate accepted-guess and answer lists. |
| Connektions | Sixteen words, four groups of four. Select/deselect words and submit exactly four. Correct groups lock with explanation; four distinct incorrect submissions end the attempt and reveal groups. Repeating an incorrect group gives feedback without another penalty. |
| Speling Bee | Seven distinct letters, required center, words of four or more letters; letters reusable. Four-letter word scores 1, longer word its length, pangram adds 7. Duplicate/invalid words score nothing. S1 completion at 70% of curated familiar goal points rounded up, with other valid ENABLE words earning separate bonus points; obscure words never inflate the completion target. Each sample has a goal pangram. Goal/bonus distinction is engineering refinement D-018; full MVP completion semantics need feedback. |
| Stranded | Themed board with adjacent horizontal/vertical/diagonal letter paths, no cell reused within a word. Accept bundled theme answers including a spanning answer; found theme paths stay marked and partition the whole board. Complete when all are found. Non-theme words do not consume attempts; no hint system or timer in S1. |
| Crossita | Valid 5x5 blocked/unblocked grid, Across/Down clues, editable crossing letters and clue selection. Complete only when every answer is correct. Optional explicit Check marks incorrect entries without deleting them; corrections remain possible. No timer/penalty in S1. |
